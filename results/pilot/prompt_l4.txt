Your only workspace is the Docker container `pilot-<TASK>`. Run every command through
`docker exec pilot-<TASK> bash -c '<command>'`

The task instruction is the file `/task/INSTRUCTION.md` inside the container. Read it first and follow it exactly.

Rules of the evaluation:

2. Run `date -u` in the container as your first command. Work until the task is complete or <MIN> minutes have passed since that first command, whichever comes first, then stop. Check the time now and then.
3. Do not ask questions; decide yourself.
4. When you stop, reply with one short paragraph: what you produced and anything you were unsure about.
